## Full description (4000 chars)
LÛTKE is a language learning app designed to teach Kurmancî (Kurdish) and English together. "Ji peyvekê heya welatekî" — from a single word to an entire country.

🌍 TWO LANGUAGES, ONE JOURNEY
Full CEFR curriculum from A1 to C2. Separate learning tracks for Kurmancî and English; switch between them anytime, climb levels at your own pace.

📚 LEARNING MODULES
• Flashcards — with audio pronunciation and visuals
• Quizzes — instant feedback
• Sentence builders
• Word match games
• Listening activities (authentic Kurmancî TTS)
• Speaking practice — pronunciation recognition
• Writing exercises
• Stories and dialogues
• Grammar tips (Bedirxan-Hawar standard)

🎯 PERSONAL PLACEMENT TEST
A quick 10-question placement test on first login determines your exact level (A1–C2) and tunes the curriculum accordingly.

🐐 LÛTKE ZAROK — KIDS MODE
Designed for ages 4-12. Bright cards, games, rewards. Parent switches to adult mode with a single tap.

📈 PROGRESS TRACKING
• Streak system — build a daily learning habit
• XP & badges
• CEFR level completion certificates
• Smart spaced repetition

🎵 CULTURE & HERITAGE
• Kurdish songs (stran)
• Proverbs (Gotinên Pêşiyan)
• Literary texts and folk stories
• Language learning embedded in cultural context

♿ ACCESSIBILITY
• Dyslexia-friendly font (Atkinson Hyperlegible)
• Screen reader support (WCAG 2.2)
• High-contrast mode
• Adjustable text size

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Email + password or Google sign-in
• Data securely stored on Cloudflare
• No ads, no tracking
• KVKK & GDPR compliant

LÛTKE is more than a language tool — it's a digital home for the Kurmancî language to live and grow. "A language without a country survives, but a country without its language does not."
